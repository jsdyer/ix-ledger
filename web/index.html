How to read an exchange record

Each member is a distinct ASN with a listed operational interface at the exchange. Speeds sum the positive interface rates in PeeringDB. Shared connections and unreported speeds limit what the sum can establish. Traffic volume and bilateral peering agreements require other measurements.

Appeared and disappeared describe the difference between the selected source dates. If an exchange is absent in one edition, its comparison has an unknown baseline.
